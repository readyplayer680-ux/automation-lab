A theory is a well-tested set of ideas that explains why or how things happen in the world. Unlike a simple guess or hunch, a true scientific theory is backed by lots of data and evidence, and people use it to make correct predictions.Main Types of TheoriesScientific theories: Backed by facts and tests (like the Theory of Evolution).Mathematical and formal theories: Built on logic and rules (like number theory).Practical theories: The core rules behind an art or skill (like music theory).Theory vs. GuessGuess/Hypothesis: A starting idea or quick thought that still needs testing.Theory: A tested framework that has survived many checks and explains a wide range of facts.If you want, let me know:Are you looking for a specific scientific theory (like gravity or relativity)?Do you need help with theory vs. practice in a specific subject?
